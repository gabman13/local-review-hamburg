# Local Review Helper MVP Kit

This folder contains a sample review collection system for a fictional Hamburg business: AlsterGlanz Reinigung.

Use it as a sales demo for local business owners. The goal is to show something concrete, not just explain the concept.

## Files

- `sample-audit.md`: editable audit text.
- `message-templates.md`: WhatsApp, email, follow-up, and review response templates.
- `review-tracker.csv`: import into Google Sheets.
- `staff-instruction-sheet.md`: editable staff routine.

Printable PDFs are generated in `output/pdf/`:

- `sample-review-gap-audit.pdf`
- `qr-review-card.pdf`
- `staff-instruction-sheet.pdf`
- `monthly-review-report.pdf`
- `review-response-templates.pdf`

## How To Customize For A Real Business

1. Replace the business name.
2. Replace the direct Google review link.
3. Replace the competitor examples with real nearby competitors.
4. Print or send the audit and QR card.
5. Import `review-tracker.csv` into Google Sheets.

## Ethical Rule

Only ask real customers for honest reviews. Do not buy fake reviews, pressure customers, or manipulate feedback.
