# Staff Instruction Sheet

Business: AlsterGlanz Reinigung

## Goal

Help satisfied real customers leave honest Google reviews more often.

## When To Ask

Ask after the customer confirms they are satisfied, ideally right after the completed job or later the same day.

## Who To Ask

Only real customers who actually used the service.

## What To Say

Thank you again for choosing us. If you were happy with our service, we would really appreciate an honest Google review. It only takes a minute, and it helps other local customers find us.

## Follow-Up Rule

If the customer does not respond, send one friendly reminder after 2-3 days. Do not pressure them.

## Ethical Rules

- Do not buy reviews.
- Do not offer rewards for positive reviews.
- Do not write reviews for customers.
- Do not ask only in a way that blocks negative feedback unfairly.
- Do not pressure customers.

## Weekly Manager Checklist

- Check whether staff are using the QR card.
- Check the review tracker every Friday.
- Send follow-ups where appropriate.
- Reply professionally to new reviews.
